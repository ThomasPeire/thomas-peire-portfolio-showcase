Notebook RunnerPrivacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to Notebook Runner on Android.

Developer: Thomas Peire

Privacy contact: peire.thomas@gmail.com

Package: com.thomaspeire.notebookrunner

Playing the game

Notebook Runner can be played without an account. There are no in-app purchases, developer-operated online leaderboards or separate gameplay analytics services. Your best distance is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. We do not sell player data. The game shows advertisements through Google Mobile Ads, as described below.

Advertisements and privacy choices

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request advertisements over the internet, shown occasionally after a run ends. Current test builds request Google's demonstration ads, which do not generate advertising revenue for us; later builds may serve live advertisements under this same policy.

Google's advertising components may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and advertisement interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID. Google uses advertising-component data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google documents encrypted transmission and its SDK data practices. Its handling and retention of data are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether privacy choices are required in your region. We initialize the advertising SDK and request ads only when that platform permits it. If required, a consent form appears before ads are shown, and a Privacy option lets you review or change your choices. If consent information or an ad cannot be obtained, you can continue playing without that advertisement. The privacy check itself communicates with Google.

You can limit ad personalization device-wide through Android's settings (Settings → Google → Ads), including resetting or deleting your advertising ID.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your best distance on your device so it can be shown between runs. The game uses Android's app storage protections. We do not have remote access to this data and do not operate a cloud-save service.

Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data according to your device and account settings. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and are separate from our game.

Retention and deletion

The locally saved best distance remains until you beat it, clear the game's storage through Android's app settings, or uninstall the game. Device backups may restore earlier game data; use your device or backup provider's settings to manage those copies. Clearing app data does not delete information already processed by Google; consult Google's privacy controls and policy for its retention and deletion options.

Support and platform services

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request, restrict access to people who need to handle it, and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or to meet applicable legal obligations. Contact the privacy email above to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence.

Google Play and Android may process installation information, diagnostics, reviews and other platform information under their own settings and policies. Google Play may make app statistics and crash reports available to developers. These platform services are separate from the game itself.

Reading this policy online

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address to deliver and protect the website under its privacy statement. This page does not include advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change, including before introducing purchases or other online services.
